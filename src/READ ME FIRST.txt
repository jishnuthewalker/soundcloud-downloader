SOUNDCLOUD DOWNLOADER
=====================

Paste a SoundCloud link (a song, a playlist, or someone's profile) and it saves
everything as MP3 files. You only run the setup file once: after that, it's an
ordinary app on your computer.

The first setup needs an internet connection and takes about 1-3 minutes.
It does NOT need an administrator password and doesn't change anything else
on your computer.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAC
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do it this way and your Mac won't show any security warnings
(double-clicking the file instead makes macOS block it):

1. Open Terminal: press Cmd+Space, type  Terminal  and press Return.
2. In the Terminal window, type  bash  followed by ONE space.
   Don't press Return yet.
3. Drag "SoundCloud Downloader (Mac).command" from Finder into the
   Terminal window. Its location appears after "bash ".
4. Click in the Terminal window and press Return.
5. Wait for "All set!". The app opens by itself, and you can close Terminal.

From now on, open "SoundCloud Downloader" from your Applications folder,
or press Cmd+Space and type SoundCloud. (Tip: drag it to your Dock.)
You won't need Terminal again.

Already double-clicked it and got "can't be opened" / "Apple could not
verify"? Click Done (NOT Move to Trash), then follow steps 1-5 above.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WINDOWS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do step 1 first and Windows won't show security warnings or block the file:

1. Unblock the zip BEFORE opening it:
     a. Right-click the downloaded zip file and choose Properties.
     b. At the bottom of the General tab, tick "Unblock". Click OK.
        (No Unblock box? That's fine, skip to step 2.)
2. Right-click the zip again, choose "Extract All...", click Extract.
3. In the folder that opens, double-click "SoundCloud Downloader (Windows)"
   (the .bat file). A black window shows the setup's progress.
4. Wait for "All set!". The app opens by itself.

From now on, open "SoundCloud Downloader" from the icon on your Desktop
or from the Start menu.

Still got a warning?
  - "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  - "Smart App Control blocked...": there's no way past this one. Delete the
    extracted folder and start again from step 1 (unblocking the zip is what
    avoids it). If you already extracted it, you can instead right-click the
    .bat file > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK, then double-click it again.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LINUX
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click "SoundCloud Downloader (Linux).sh" and choose
   "Run as a Program" (on some systems: Properties > Permissions >
   "Allow executing file as program" first).
   OR open a terminal, type  bash  and a space, drag the file in, press Enter.
2. Wait for "All set!". The app opens by itself.
3. From now on, open "SoundCloud Downloader" from your applications menu.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
USING IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. On SoundCloud, open the song, playlist or profile you want and copy its link
   (click Share > Copy link, or copy the address from your browser).
2. In the app, click Paste, then "Download MP3s".
3. It tells you what it found and asks before downloading.
4. When it's done, it can open the folder for you. Songs go to
   Music > SoundCloud unless you choose another folder with Browse.

If some songs fail, you'll see a list with the reason and a
"Retry failed" button. Running the same link again only downloads songs you
don't already have.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SIGNING IN (OPTIONAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You don't need to sign in for normal public songs. Sign in if you want to:
  - download your own likes (a "Download my likes" button appears),
  - download private tracks or playlists you have access to,
  - get full Go+ tracks (only if your account has SoundCloud Go+).

How:
1. Click "Sign in..." in the app.
2. Click "Open SoundCloud" and sign in in your normal web browser
   (skip this if you're already signed in there).
3. Pick that browser from the list (or leave "Find it automatically") and
   click Connect.
     Mac: if it asks to allow access to "Chrome Safe Storage" (or similar),
          type your Mac login password and click "Always Allow".
          Safari can't be used; use Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Brave.
     Windows: Firefox works best. Chrome and Edge lock their sign-in on
          Windows; if Connect fails with them, use the other way below.
4. Done: the app shows "Signed in as <your name>".

The other way: in the sign-in window click "Show me another way..." and follow
the steps to copy your sign-in token from the browser and paste it in.

Your password is never seen or stored by this app. It only keeps the sign-in
token (on this computer, readable only by you). Click "Sign out" in the app to
remove it. If downloads say your sign-in expired, sign out and sign in again.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEMS?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The app won't open, or says part of it is missing: run the setup file again,
  the same way as the first time. It repairs itself and keeps your settings.
- "Rate-limited by SoundCloud": wait a few minutes, lower "Parallel downloads",
  then click "Retry failed".
- Updates are automatic: every time you open the app it spends a few seconds
  "Checking for updates" and installs the newest version of itself and of
  everything it uses (SoundCloud changes sometimes break older versions).
  Keep the internet on when you open it. If downloads suddenly all fail,
  close the app and open it again.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UNINSTALL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Delete the app folder and the shortcut. Your downloaded music is not touched.
  Mac:     ~/Library/Application Support/SoundCloudDownloader
           and "SoundCloud Downloader" in Applications
  Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\SoundCloudDownloader
           and the Desktop / Start menu shortcuts
  Linux:   ~/.local/share/SoundCloudDownloader
           and ~/.local/share/applications/soundcloud-downloader.desktop


Please only download music you have the right to download.
